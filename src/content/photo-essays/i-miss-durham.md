Certain towns keep a shape in you long after you leave.

## The light

Durham light is southern light, but filtered through tobacco warehouses and brick. It does something to late afternoons that I haven't seen anywhere else.

## What I miss

The Lakewood YMCA pool at 6am. The Cocoa Cinnamon on Geer. Biking up Broad Street on a fall morning when the air first turns.
