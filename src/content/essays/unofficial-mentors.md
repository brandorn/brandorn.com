Most of my mentors have never met me. They're writers and designers whose work I've read carefully enough, and for long enough, that their habits have started to rub off.

## The library list

A short, incomplete list of people whose blog posts I re-read when I'm stuck:

- Frank Chimero
- Jenny Odell
- Maggie Appleton
- Robin Sloan

## How it works

You borrow someone's way of paying attention. Over time it becomes your own, with your own fingerprints on it.
