There's a phrase I come back to: *care for the world, and make new good things*. It's two halves, and I think the second half gets most of the attention in our industry while the first half does most of the work.

Caring for the world is maintenance. It's the bug fix, the accessibility pass, the refactor nobody asked for. It's noticing the thing that's almost right and spending an afternoon making it right.

Making new good things is the part we put on our portfolios. But new good things that don't come from a posture of care tend to age badly.
