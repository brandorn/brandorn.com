Pandemic year. I spent a lot of it looking down.

## The ground is a composition

Leaves don't arrange themselves, except they absolutely do. Every patch of wet sidewalk in November is a still life.

## A practice

Carry a camera. Walk slower. Take the picture, even if it feels silly.
