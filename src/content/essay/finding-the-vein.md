Every project has a vein of real, useful work buried somewhere inside it. Your job, early on, is to find it.

## Hits and misses

You'll mine in a lot of wrong directions first. That's fine. The wrong directions are how you learn the shape of the deposit.

## Signals

The vein usually shows up as a question users keep asking in slightly different ways, or a workflow they've built themselves out of spit and duct tape. Pay attention to the workarounds.
